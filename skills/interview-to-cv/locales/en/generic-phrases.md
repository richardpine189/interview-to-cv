# Generic phrases (en)

Phrases that make a CV summary or cover letter sound generic. The generic-language check (DESIGN §9) flags every match (case-insensitive, ignoring small variations) and asks the user to replace it with a specific fact or delete it. A match is a warning, not an error: a phrase backed by evidence in the same sentence can stay.

Add newly overused phrases at each semi-annual review.

## Openers

- I am writing to express my interest in
- I am writing to apply for
- I was excited to see your job posting
- Please accept this letter as my application
- I would like to be considered for
- My name is … and I am applying

## Self-description without evidence

- hard-working
- highly motivated
- results-driven / results-oriented
- detail-oriented
- dynamic professional
- self-starter
- go-getter
- team player
- passionate about
- proven track record
- excellent communication skills
- strong work ethic
- think outside the box
- wear many hats
- fast learner / quick learner
- out-of-the-box thinker
- seasoned professional
- synergy
- best of breed

## Inflated or empty claims

- unique opportunity
- perfect fit / ideal candidate
- I believe I would be a great asset
- I am confident that my skills and experience
- add value
- make a difference
- leverage my skills
- extensive experience (without years or examples)
- various projects / various technologies
- responsible for (as a bullet opener — describe the result instead)
- duties included

- I am thrilled / I am excited to
- delve into
- in today's fast-paced world
- ever-evolving landscape
- a testament to
- seamlessly
- cutting-edge
- spearheaded (when the user did not lead)
- passionate about leveraging
- align with your mission
- tapestry
- navigate the complexities

## Closings

- Thank you for your time and consideration
- I look forward to hearing from you
- Please do not hesitate to contact me
- I would welcome the opportunity to discuss
- Feel free to reach out

Closings are allowed once per letter; flag them only when the closing paragraph contains nothing else.
